How Northwind Retail cut weekly reporting from 3 days to 20 minutes

A 400-store European retailer replaced six manual spreadsheets with a single live Orbit workspace — freeing their data team to work on forecasting instead of formatting.

The challenge

Six spreadsheets, one very tired analyst

Every Monday, a single analyst pulled point-of-sale exports from six regional systems, reconciled currencies by hand, and rebuilt the same set of pivot tables before the leadership stand-up. A single formatting mistake meant a re-run — and sometimes a missed meeting.

Store managers had no way to see their own numbers between reports, so small problems — a slow POS terminal, a stockout — went unnoticed for days.

The solution

One event pipeline, live dashboards for every store

Northwind connected their POS and inventory systems to Orbit's ingestion API in an afternoon. Store-level dashboards were built once using Orbit's saved views, then cloned automatically for all 400+ locations with the correct filters applied.

Store managers now get a live view refreshed every five minutes. Regional directors get a daily digest. Leadership gets the weekly roll-up automatically, with zero manual assembly.

Rollout timeline

  • 1 Day 1 — Kickoff & data mapping

    Orbit's onboarding team mapped POS and inventory fields to Orbit's event schema.

  • 2 Day 3 — First live dashboard

    A pilot dashboard went live for two flagship stores in Amsterdam and Berlin.

  • 3 Day 6 — Template cloned to all stores

    The saved view was cloned across all 400+ locations with store-level filters.

  • 4 Day 9 — Full rollout & training

    Store managers were trained; the manual reporting process was retired for good.

"Orbit didn't just replace our dashboards — it changed how fast we can make decisions. What used to be a Friday-afternoon spreadsheet ritual is now a five-minute check before stand-up."

Priya Nandakumar

VP of Data, Northwind Retail
