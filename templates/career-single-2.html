Robotics Software Engineer

Remote — US · Full-time · $150k – $190k + equity

Apply Now

You'll write the software that lets our inspection robots navigate industrial environments autonomously — from perception and planning to the control systems that keep them safe. This role sits at the intersection of robotics, distributed systems, and real-world hardware.

  • + Develop navigation and path-planning software for autonomous robots
  • + Build tooling to simulate and test robot behavior at scale
  • + Work directly with hardware engineers on integration
  • + Debug issues in the field alongside our deployment team
  • + 4+ years building software for robotics or autonomous systems
  • + Strong C++ or Python, comfortable with ROS/ROS2
  • + Experience with SLAM, motion planning, or perception
  • + Willingness to travel occasionally for field testing
  • + Equity in an early-stage, well-funded company
  • + 100% covered health, dental & vision
  • + $2,000 annual learning & development budget
  • + Unlimited PTO with a 3-week minimum
